# Portfolio

Muhammad Abu Bakar's personal site: the writing and the work published to show what they build and how they think.

## Language

### Content

**Post**:
A piece of writing published on the blog, such as a tutorial, guide or essay. A Post may be about one Project.
_Avoid_: Blog, article, doc

**Project**:
A piece of work Bakar built, shown as a card with its stack and links.
_Avoid_: Work, portfolio item, app

**Case Study**:
An optional long-form write-up attached to a Project, explaining the problem, the role Bakar played and the decisions behind it.
_Avoid_: Project page, project post

**Featured Project**:
A Project picked to appear on the home page and in the navigation.
_Avoid_: Highlighted, pinned

**Stack**:
The technologies a Project was built with.
_Avoid_: Category, tech, tools

### Publishing

**Draft**:
A Post or Project that lives with the published content but is not shown on the site.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, WIP, hidden

**Publish**:
The deliberate act of sending finished content to the live site.
_Avoid_: Sync, deploy, push

**Vault**:
The folder of content Bakar opens in a writing app; it is the same content the site publishes.
_Avoid_: Notes, workspace

### Distribution

**Share Card**:
The title, description and image a platform shows when a link to the site is shared.
_Avoid_: Link preview, OG card, social card

**Cross-post**:
A full copy of a Post republished on another platform that credits the site as the original.
_Avoid_: Repost, syndication, mirror
